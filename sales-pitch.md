## What We Actually Do

### 1. Build the ontology
We map your operation into a model an agent can work with:
- **Objects:** customers, leads, jobs, appointments, invoices
- **Actions:** respond, qualify, quote, book, reschedule, escalate
- **Rules:** when to auto-send, when to route to a human, what "urgent" means in your business

This is the layer that makes everything else possible. It's also the layer nobody else builds.

### 2. Forward-deploy an operator
An AI agent — deployed inside your workflow, not bolted on — that:
- Reads every inbound signal (email, form, call)
- Reasons against your ontology (who is this, what do they need, what's the rule)
- Takes action (reply, book, update, escalate)
- Writes back to your systems of record

### 3. Run it in production
We don't hand you a platform. We operate the system for you — monitoring, tuning, handling edge cases, extending the ontology as your business changes.

**Phase 1 — Entry:** One workflow, modeled end-to-end. Usually lead response, scheduling, or follow-ups.
**Phase 2 — Expansion:** More objects, more actions, more autonomy. Multi-agent orchestration. Deeper writeback.

We earn the right to expand. We don't sell complexity upfront.

## Vocabulary the sales team should use

- **Ontology** — the model of your business objects, actions, and rules. "Your business runs on an implicit ontology. We make it explicit."
- **Legibility** — the property of data an agent can reason over. "Your CRM has the data. It's not legible to AI."
- **Forward-deployed operator** — the agent we embed in the workflow. Not a tool. Not a chatbot.
- **System of action** (vs. system of record) — the thing that actually does the work, not just logs it.
- **Operational advantage** — what you get: sub-60-second response, 24/7 coverage, zero dropped handoffs.
- **Ground truth** — every action logged, auditable, written back. No shadow databases.
- **Writeback** — results flow back into your existing systems. We don't replace anything.

## Objection Handling — General

### "Why can't I just use ChatGPT?"
> "ChatGPT is a model. We build the ontology around it. ChatGPT doesn't know who your customers are, what your rules are, or when to escalate. An agent with no model of your business writes nice emails into a void. We give it a model and a workflow to operate inside of."

### "Will AI replace my employees?"
> "No — the agent handles the operational work your team hates. Your people do what humans are good at: judgment, relationship, exceptions. The agent handles the routine. That's amplification, not replacement."

### "What if it makes mistakes?"
> "It will, at first. That's why the ontology has explicit human-in-the-loop checkpoints for anything high-stakes. Every action is logged and auditable. We catch and fix fast, and the system gets sharper every week."

### "Is my data safe?"
> "Yes. Scoped permissions — the agent only touches the objects it needs. We don't train on your data. Every action is logged with full audit trail. You own the ontology and the writeback."

### "I'm not technical. Can I use this?"
> "That's the whole point. You describe how your business actually works. We turn that into the ontology. We handle the engineering."

### "This seems expensive."
> "Compare it to hiring. A full-time admin is $40K+ with benefits, works 40 hours a week, calls in sick. Tend is $24K/year, operates 24/7, never misses a lead, and compounds over time as the ontology gets richer."

### "I've tried AI tools before and they didn't work."
> "Most AI tools are self-serve — you're on your own to figure out how to plug them into your operation. That's the missing layer. We build that layer. We forward-deploy the operator. We run it. You just see the output."

You bring the personal touch — we make sure you get the chance to."

**"I already have a CRM that does follow-ups."**
> "CRMs send templated drips. We deploy an agent that actually reads the reply, reasons against your ontology, and responds. It's the difference between a workflow engine and an operator. One logs; the other acts."
